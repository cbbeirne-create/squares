# Public website content draft

This document accompanies the first coded draft of the public marketing pages. It is intended for content review before brand, domain and commercial decisions are finalised.

## Proposed page map

| Route | Primary audience | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `/` | Clubs and supporters | Explain the concept and direct club decision-makers deeper into the site |
| `/how-it-works` | Clubs and supporters | Explain the full campaign journey in plain language |
| `/for-clubs` | Club committees and campaign teams | Explain controls, responsibilities and operational fit |
| `/pricing` | Club decision-makers | Describe the proposal structure without inventing prices |
| `/faq` | Clubs and supporters | Resolve practical, payment and moderation questions |
| `/contact` | Qualified club enquiries | Set expectations for the first campaign conversation |

## Content principles used

- Lead with belonging and legacy, then explain the technology.
- Avoid unverified performance claims, testimonials and client logos.
- Distinguish confirmed product behaviour from decisions still to be made.
- Use Irish and British English conventions.
- Keep paragraphs short enough for mobile reading.
- Describe Stripe accurately without suggesting that payment processing is free.
- Treat the current product name and all domain references as provisional.

## Decisions needed before final copy

1. Final product and domain name.
2. Confirmed commercial model and whether any prices will be public.
3. Responsibility for Stripe processing fees and refunds.
4. Length and cost of post-campaign archival hosting.
5. Support included before, during and after launch.
6. Primary contact email and enquiry handling process.
7. Legal entity name, registered details, privacy policy and terms.
8. Approved pilot example, imagery, testimonial and performance figures when available.

## Suggested public URL model

Use `chosen-domain/club-slug` as the canonical supporter address. Keep `/board/club-slug` as the internal application route until the public routing change is approved and implemented.
